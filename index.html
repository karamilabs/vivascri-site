04 Privacy

This site practices the privacy it preaches.

Vivascri is built local-first. The app you download needs no account and sends nothing. The only network calls are ones you start yourself, like fetching a model or adding a paper by link, plus a licence check for paid tiers.

No account for the free app

Opt-in cloud AI, clearly labelled
Connect Claude or GPT with your own key and the app shows it while it is on. Off by default. Paid-tier retrieval still runs locally.
No analytics, no cookies

07 FAQ

Straight answers.

The free tier is free, local, and offline, with no account. The paid tiers add stronger retrieval models that also run on your machine; they come with a licence key from our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, which the app verifies online occasionally. You never need a paid tier to write and cite.
No. Writing, your PDF library, search, citations, and AI drafting all run on your machine. The only network calls are ones you start yourself: downloading an AI model, or adding a paper by DOI or link, which fetches a free open-access copy. If you connect a cloud AI (Claude or GPT) in Settings, your text goes to that provider; that is off by default and clearly marked. Paid tiers verify their licence key online now and then, sending the key and nothing about your documents. No telemetry, nothing else uploaded.
Twice. Once to download the app, and once on first launch, when it fetches a small AI model (about 1 GB, one time). After that, writing, citing, and AI drafting work fully offline. Adding a paper by DOI or link needs a connection; dropping in PDFs you already have does not. Paid tiers also need to reach the internet occasionally to confirm their licence key.

Different tools. Zotero manages a reference library; Vivascri is where you write. It keeps its own local library of the PDFs you cite, pulls clean open-access copies from a DOI or arXiv link, imports .bib and .ris files, and formats references in APA, MLA, Chicago, or Harvard. Already on Zotero? Export a .bib and import it here. Just need to write a properly cited paper? Vivascri alone covers it.
Every citation is tied to a real passage in a PDF you own. Click one and Vivascri opens that exact page and highlights where it came from. The AI can only cite sources retrieved from your own library, and the export gate refuses any citation that is missing its real source data, and names it. A made-up citation never lands in your paper silently.
